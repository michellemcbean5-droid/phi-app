# App Store Assets Guide — PHI Mobile

This document provides guidelines for creating screenshots, copy templates, and keyword strategies for Google Play Store and Apple App Store listings.

---

## Screenshot Guidelines

### Required Sizes

#### Google Play Store

| Type | Size | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Phone screenshots | 1080×1920 or 1242×2208 | PNG or JPEG |
| Tablet (7-inch) | 1080×1920 | PNG or JPEG |
| Tablet (10-inch) | 2560×1600 | PNG or JPEG |
| Feature graphic | 1024×500 | PNG or JPEG |

#### Apple App Store

| Device | Size | Format |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone 6.7" (iPhone 15 Pro Max) | 1290×2796 | PNG |
| iPhone 6.5" (iPhone 14 Pro Max) | 1284×2778 | PNG |
| iPhone 5.5" (iPhone 8 Plus) | 1242×2208 | PNG |
| iPad Pro 12.9" (6th gen) | 2048×2732 | PNG |
| iPad Pro 12.9" (2nd gen) | 2048×2732 | PNG |

### Screenshot Content Strategy

Use **5 screenshots maximum** per device type. Each should tell a story:

1. **Hero / Value Proposition**
   - Show the main dashboard with KPI cards.
   - Headline: "AI-Powered Trucking Intelligence"
   - Subtitle: "Find better loads. Track profit. Stay compliant."

2. **Load Board**
   - Show filtered loads with Diamond/Gold/Standard scoring.
   - Headline: "Smart Load Scoring"
   - Subtitle: "Diamond loads = highest profit potential"

3. **AI Command Center**
   - Show the Claude chat interface.
   - Headline: "Your AI Co-Pilot"
   - Subtitle: "Ask anything about loads, routes, or DOT rules"

4. **Earnings & Profit**
   - Show the net profit hero card and yearly projection.
   - Headline: "Know Your Numbers"
   - Subtitle: "Real-time profit tracking & yearly forecasts"

5. **Compliance**
   - Show HOS hours and DOT audit report.
   - Headline: "Stay DOT Compliant"
   - Subtitle: "Automatic HOS tracking & audit-ready reports"

### Design Tips

- Use the PHI brand colors: Royal Blue (#0057FF), Sunshine Yellow (#FFD600), Money Green (#00C853).
- Keep text minimal and readable at small sizes.
- Use device frames for a polished look.
- Highlight one feature per screenshot.
- Maintain consistent spacing and typography.

- Load board
- Freight dispatch
- HOS tracker
- ELD compliance
- Truck driver app
- Owner operator
- Fleet management
- Route planner
- Profit tracker

### Secondary Keywords

- DOT compliance
- Hours of service

- Deadhead calculator

- Weigh stations
- Fuel stops
- AI dispatch

### Long-Tail Keywords

- Best app for truck drivers
- Free load board for owner operators
- HOS logbook app
- Trucking profit calculator
- Fleet management software for small fleets
- AI trucking dispatcher
- DOT audit preparation app
- Document scanner for truckers

### Keyword Placement Tips

1. **App Name:** Include your strongest keyword (e.g., "PHI - Prince Haul Intelligence: Trucking & Fleet").
2. **Subtitle:** Use 2-3 keywords naturally.
3. **Description:** Repeat primary keywords 3-5 times, but write for humans first.
4. **Keyword field (iOS):** Use all 100 characters. No spaces — separate with commas.
   - Example: `trucking,load board,dispatch,HOS,ELD,owner operator,fleet,profit,route,AI`

## Promo Video Guidelines

### Google Play Promo Video

- **Length:** 30-90 seconds
- **Resolution:** 1920×1080 (16:9)
- **Format:** MP4, H.264
- **Content:**
  - 0-5s: Hook — "What if your phone was your best dispatcher?"
  - 5-30s: Feature showcase — Dashboard → Load Board → AI Chat → Earnings
  - 30-45s: Social proof — "Trusted by 10,000+ drivers"
  - 45-60s: CTA — "Download free. Upgrade when you're ready."

### App Store Preview Video

- **Length:** 15-30 seconds
- **Resolutions:** Match screenshot sizes above
- **No audio required** (most users watch muted)
- Use text overlays to explain features

## Review Preparation

### App Store Review Guidelines Checklist

- [ ] App is fully functional with no crashes
- [ ] All placeholder content removed
- [ ] In-app purchases are testable with sandbox accounts
- [ ] App does not contain hidden features or undocumented functionality
- [ ] Privacy policy URL is live and accessible
- [ ] App does not request unnecessary permissions
- [ ] Sign-in works with provided demo account

### Google Play Review Checklist

- [ ] Content rating questionnaire completed
- [ ] Target audience declared (18+)
- [ ] Data safety form completed accurately
- [ ] Privacy policy URL provided
- [ ] App meets Google Play's Families Policy (if applicable)
- [ ] No restricted content (gambling, illegal substances, etc.)
