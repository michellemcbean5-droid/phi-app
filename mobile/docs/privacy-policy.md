# Privacy Policy — Prince Haul Intelligence (PHI)

**Effective Date:** July 8, 2026

**Last Updated:** July 8, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Prince Haul Intelligence ("PHI," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use the PHI mobile application (the "App").

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information:** Name, email address, phone number, company name (if applicable).
- **Vehicle & Equipment Data:** Truck/VIN details, equipment specifications, maintenance records.
- **Driver Preferences:** Route preferences, load types, HOS settings.
- **Documents:** Bills of lading, proof of delivery, inspection reports (uploaded via camera or gallery).
- **Payment Information:** We do not store full payment card details. All payments are processed securely by Apple App Store, Google Play, Stripe, or RevenueCat.
- **API Keys:** If you choose to use your own AI provider keys (BYOK), they are stored securely on your device using native keychain/keystore.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Location Data:** GPS coordinates for route planning, deadhead calculations, and load proximity alerts. We collect location only when the App is in use or with your explicit permission for background tracking.
- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address.
- **Usage Data:** App screens visited, features used, session duration, crash logs.
- **Analytics Data:** Aggregated, anonymized data for improving the App.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide and maintain the App's core functionality (load booking, routing, compliance tracking).
- Calculate routes, deadhead miles, and estimated arrival times.
- Send push notifications for load alerts, HOS warnings, and document reminders.
- Process subscriptions and manage billing through Apple, Google, Stripe, or RevenueCat.
- Improve App performance and user experience through analytics and crash reporting.
- Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We **do not** sell your personal information to third parties.

---

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process personal data based on:

- **Contractual necessity:** To provide the services you subscribed to.
- **Consent:** For location tracking, push notifications, and marketing communications (where applicable).
- **Legitimate interests:** For analytics, fraud prevention, and App security.
- **Legal obligation:** To comply with DOT regulations, tax laws, or court orders.

## 5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We share data only with trusted service providers who help us operate the App:

| Third Party | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---|---|---|
| Apple App Store / Google Play | Subscription billing | Purchase receipts, subscription status |
| RevenueCat | Subscription management | Anonymous purchase events |
| Stripe | Payment processing (web) | Payment tokens (not full card data) |
| Sentry | Crash reporting | Error logs, device info (anonymized) |
| Firebase / Google Analytics | Usage analytics | Aggregated, anonymized usage patterns |
| Anthropic (Claude API) | AI features | Chat messages, route queries (when using managed AI) |
| Google Maps / OpenRouteService | Routing & geocoding | Origin/destination coordinates |

All third-party processors are bound by data processing agreements that require them to protect your data and use it only for the specified purposes.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Active accounts:** We retain your data for as long as your account is active.
- **Deleted accounts:** Upon account deletion, we delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., tax or DOT records).
- **Documents:** Uploaded documents are retained until you delete them or close your account.
- **Analytics data:** Aggregated analytics may be retained indefinitely in anonymized form.

## 8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- **Encryption in transit:** All data transmitted between the App and our servers uses TLS 1.2+.
- **Encryption at rest:** Sensitive data is encrypted using AES-256.
- **Secure storage:** API keys and tokens are stored in the device keychain (iOS) or Keystore (Android).
- **Access controls:** Server infrastructure is protected by role-based access controls and MFA.
- **Regular audits:** We conduct security assessments and vulnerability scans.

Despite our efforts, no system is completely secure. If you believe your data has been compromised, please contact us immediately.
